# No Needs Pawn

> A RimWorld 1.6 mod that adds a custom humanlike race that feels **no pain, never sleeps, and only needs food**.

They are the ultimate tireless workers and soldiers — capable of **24/7 warfare, construction, and expansion** without ever experiencing a mental break.

This mod also includes a **"Transform to no-needs" ability** that lets your pawns convert others into this race. The transformation spreads — those transformed can, in turn, transform others.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🧬 **Custom Humanlike Race** — Only needs Food. No pain, no mood, no sleep, no recreation, no joy. The perfect 24/7 worker or soldier.
- 🔮 **Transformation Ability** — A "Transform to no-needs" ability that turns any pawn (vanilla humans, colonists, etc.) into a no-needs pawn.
- 🌊 **Spreading Effect** — Transformed pawns gain the ability to transform others.
- ⚙️ **Mod Settings** — Change the ability's cooldown (Instant / Hours / Days) and pick from **8 different colony colors** for starting pawn gear.
- 🧠 **Trait Stripping** — Automatically removes all mood-related traits (Sanguine, Depressive, Volatile, Misandrist, etc.) from your pawns.
- 📖 **Custom Scenarios** — Includes 3 story-driven scenarios: **Lone Survivor** (1 pawn), **The Remnants** (3 pawns), and **The Final Vanguard** (5 pawns).

## 🛠️ Installation

1. Unzip the contents into your `RimWorld/Mods/` folder.
2. Activate the mod in the mod menu.
3. Make sure **Harmony** and **HAR** load **before** this mod.

---

## 💡 Tips

> ⚠️ **Difficulty Warning:** This mod makes the game significantly easier. To keep it fun, **RAISE THE THREAT SCALE and DIFFICULTY**. The fights will never stop, and neither will your pawns.

> 🎮 **Using on an existing save:**
> 1. Load your world and enable **Dev Mode**.
> 2. Open the debug menu and go to **Add Hediff**.
> 3. Apply `NoNeedsTransformationHediff` to one of your pawns.
> 4. They will become a no-needs pawn. You can then use the transform ability to spread it to others.
